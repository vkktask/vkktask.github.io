03

PAARA — the flagship contribution

PAARA (Privacy-Aware AI Risk Architecture) is my original contribution to the field: a practitioner methodology for assessing AI connectors as privacy- and security-relevant processing enablers. It doesn't replace a GDPR DPIA, an EU AI Act FRIA, or a security review — it adds the connector-layer module those processes are missing, and aligns the findings across all of them.

A

Connector inventory

Catalogue every integration, OAuth scope, Graph permission, and data source reachable at query time.

B

WP29 nine-criteria

Map each connector against the DPIA trigger criteria — most deployments trigger five at once.

C

Permission risk register

Score each scope against data minimisation; flag tenant-wide and application-level grants.

D / E

Retrieval & injection model

Where connector access exceeds the user's; where ingested content redirects behaviour.

F

Insider-risk amplification

The risk no authority addresses: the connector used exactly as designed collapses access friction.

G

Cross-framework alignment

One assessment satisfying GDPR Art. 35, EU AI Act Art. 27, ISO 42005, NIST AI 600-1, OWASP LLM Top 10.
